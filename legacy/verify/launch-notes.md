# Launch fidelity follow-up

Build: `npx tsc --noEmit && npx vite build --outDir dist-perf`; preview: `npx vite preview --outDir dist-perf --port 4175`. All scripts accept `VERIFY_URL=http://localhost:4175/`.

Runtime profiles in the **same build**:
- `?physics=legacy`: reconstructs the saved `perf-before/physics.ts.txt` stepping and solver settings (60 Hz; 48/12 above 512 colliders, 12/4 otherwise; uncapped catch-up). The original core grouping is retained. Render/upload/rewind optimizations stay enabled in every profile to isolate physics and avoid concurrent lighting differences.
- `?physics=economy`: the previous performance fix, preserved in `perf-current/physics.ts.txt`: crowded 4/1, one tick per render frame, two in small worlds, whole overdue ticks discarded.
- `?physics=launch` (default): fresh crowded shards receive 44 additional solver iterations above the base 4; Rapier propagates these through each contact island. PGS is 12 during the burst. Both ramp down over fixed ticks 36–48 (0.6–0.8 seconds). Three-tick bounded catch-up during launch, previous economical caps afterward. Small worlds retain their original 12/4 solver. The new launch scheduling is explicitly enabled by the aquarium through separateLaunch; Lab and generic library users retain their existing stepping caps. No variable timestep or wall-clock-dependent solver decisions.

The full-world 48/12 launch trial still produced 100–220 ms frames. The final implementation confines extra iterations to fresh bodies, gives shards a brief mutual-collision grace period (0.2 seconds small / 0.4 crowded), and retains collisions against fixed ground and surviving panes throughout. Earlier trials restoring mutual contacts at 0.6 seconds left persistent pile jitter; restoring earlier lets those contacts settle naturally. There is still a transient contact cost during the ramp, so median FPS alone does not mean every frame meets 16.7 ms. Raw per-frame timing retains those spikes.

The saved legacy code already welded the pristine burst's 70 impact cells into 16 compound bodies. Pair-of-body metrics cannot detect those internal welded cells. The new pristine Space release preserves all 70 visible cells but gives each a body (132 total versus 78); the four-wall flow retains 285 bodies. Geometry, material, normal/radial velocity law, mass-independent impulse calculation, spin law, and one-event-frame impulse sequence are unchanged. The independent cells sample that same spatial velocity law individually, making the core visibly more energetic than the available legacy reference. This is an intentional change to separation, not a claim of identical trajectories or pixel identity.

No sleep threshold, contact skin, prediction distance or cached-pose policy changed. Native sleeping remains responsible for rest. The existing Run N deterministic trajectory assertion is unchanged; an additional crowded fixture verifies bit-identical trajectories across render chunking through the fidelity ramp, restoration of collision groups, and the three-step backlog cap. No expected trajectories were rewritten.

`node verify/launch-compare.mjs` measures legacy/economy/launch, pristine single-wall Space at the default camera, and the exact four-wall flow from edges.mjs. `PROFILES` and `CASE` optionally narrow trials. `--shots` captures each requested frame on a fresh page, avoiding readback stalls affecting later captures. Images are `perf-before/launch-{01,03,06,10}.png` and matching `perf-after/` paths. Image times are scene elapsed time, matching runtime.mjs; actual capture times are retained in launch-shots.txt. The screenshots isolate physics profiles using identical final lighting and rendering.

`perf-{before,current,after}/launch-{single,four}.json` retains every frame for the first 1.5 seconds and beyond: wall age, scene age, physics age/tick count, body-weighted speed p50/p90 (scene units/s; populations are reported because independent core cells increase the single-wall body count), angular speed p50/p90 (rad/s), asleep count, effective solver iterations, and linked pairs (world centres closer than 0.25, linear velocities closer than 0.15 units/s). Linked pairs include old crack fragments resting before Space; they are not all a fresh-release defect. `radial` is the mean absolute velocity projection fraction along each shard's initial in-pane radial direction; `radialOutward` is its signed counterpart. Gravity and contact change both after release. Zero velocity contributes zero. `at` samples the nearest scene frame; `wallAt` samples the nearest wall-time frame. Slow legacy frames make the clocks diverge: compare physics age as well as the requested timestamp.

FPS summaries in launch-compare use actual performance.now() frame intervals over wall-time 0.1–1.5 seconds (airborne) and 8–10 seconds (settled). The script also saves the scene-window FPS. First-half-second step counts explicitly distinguish wall time from scene time. Impulse frame IDs and counts prove the launch is not staggered. The opt-in probe's pair scan adds diagnostic overhead; no probe collection runs normally.

`PHYSICS=legacy node verify/four-wall-perf.mjs legacy` and `node verify/four-wall-perf.mjs after` measure CPU categories and traced GC without the shard-pair probe. See perf-notes.md for category definitions and trace alignment; its original before/after evidence describes the earlier performance task, while launch-* evidence describes this follow-up.

Validation note: the first full Lab run found L3 rest rotation 0.01898 rad (limit 0.01). A focused same-build comparison found intermittent residual motion with the new three-tick cap outside the aquarium; the previous cap was clean in both trials. The new scheduling is now restricted to the aquarium opt-in. The subsequent complete Lab run passed all levels, with zero L3 displacement, rotation below 6e-8 rad and zero awake bodies at five seconds. The original Run N assertion and every Lab tolerance are unchanged. Initial failure and diagnostic logs remain available for audit. This scope correction does not change the aquarium path used for the launch measurements or images.

Final validation: build and all four suites passed; runtime.mjs ran once, last. The four-wall timing-only run measured median 59.88 FPS airborne / 60.61 settled, with zero awake shards in every recorded 5.0–5.1 s sample. The launch-pair probe measured 59.88 / 59.88 FPS; its 0.1–1.5 s wall-time window retained a 78.6 ms p95 and 89.0 ms worst frame. Median targets are met, but the contact ramp is not uniformly 60 FPS. Full verbatim metrics and selected suite PASS lines are in launch-evidence.txt; per-frame JSON and the eight images remain in perf-before/perf-after (previous economical metrics in perf-current). Final visual inspection shows a distinctly separated impact fan, stronger than the saved legacy compound core; no glass material or visible fracture cell was removed.
