# Run F evidence

## Changes and proof

1. **Branding:** the 160px logo dominated the masthead. It now uses 96/80/72px breakpoints and a 14px heading gap. Runtime asserts all three widths; `../pass-default.png` shows the result.
2. **Spoiler:** the topic and page title disclosed the technique. The topic now blurs/reveals in 250ms on hover, keyboard focus and touch toggle, with neutral accessible naming while hidden. Hover CSS is limited to hover-capable devices. Runtime checks title, focus, pinned reveal, and two touch taps; the Lab text is unchanged.
3. **Floor:** inspection found two unlit surfaces, with the contact plane only .003 units above a 10,000-unit floor. Neither receives sun shadows, ruling out the directional shadow map for this floor wedge. The original blink did not reproduce in the stationary ten-frame capture; near-coplanar depth precision remains the suspected trigger, not a proven reproduction. The contact shadow now belongs to the floor's own fragment shader, removing competing geometry. `shadow-final/` contains ten frames while the sun and shaders animate: a 56×56 floor region changes by at most 1/255 per channel (mean .0176). See `shadow-final.json`. `shadow-probe.mjs` is a separate synthetic low-angle depth-plane comparison, not a reproduction of the user's screenshot.
4. **Shafts:** six intersecting sheets caused stripes, hard cuts and light over the island. One water box now performs a 16-step jittered single-scattering raymarch, with tank/tilted-water intersections, opaque-depth termination, .08-unit wall fades, depth attenuation, and two terrain-height tests toward the sun. All four camera captures were reviewed. Runtime measures a 60 FPS median at 1920×1080 / 1.5 DPR for each preset, with a 55 FPS scheduling tolerance assertion.
5. **Resting glass:** the baseline four-wall probe retained 77 awake bodies at eight seconds; between eight and ten seconds it moved .0128101 units and rotated .112342 radians. Collider inset, thinner hulls, pane-end clearance, floor damping, one-second CCD expiry, quiet-body sleep and a six-second fixed-body deadline now produce zero displacement and 5.96e-8 radians maximum rotation. See `settle-before.json`, `settle-after.json`; `settle-before.mjs --before` uses the archived original source. Runtime independently asserts the same limits on every displayed shard.
6. **Reset:** the old action snapped to pristine. A bounded 30 Hz / 20-second history now replays backwards with eased position/quaternion interpolation, water height/normal, puddles, ribbons and visibility. Slot poses provide a landing even after old frames leave the ring. Particles clear; crack lines fade rather than geometrically retract. The exact old cleanup runs at the end. `../rewind-mid.png` and runtime prove real shard movement and final height=2.59, cracks=0, bodies=0. Edges verifies mid-spill water rise, paused physics, reverse audio, slow motion and second-Reset skip. Visual time stays continuous after rewind.
7. **Audio:** muted defaults, leading silence, a weak envelope, lazy decode and awaiting device resume delayed the hit. Suspended preload, gesture resume, detected onset trimming, gain .9 crack / 1.0 shatter, randomized crack rate and audible-section bubble looping replace those paths. Offsets are .403958s and .380792s (including 10ms pre-onset). The diagnostic samples the actual render frame when source.start is called; runtime caught and removed a three-frame resume wait, then passed same-frame crack/shatter assertions. Library tests cover reset during pending decode/resume and already-scheduled playback before resume completes.
8. **Visual review:** a gap and bright emissive rim made the water surface float; nested transparent layers and strong underside reflections flattened the island. The water now meets the glass, with a narrower/dimmer meniscus, restrained underside/sky reflections and less face-on glass/volume opacity. Reviewed default, front-left, front and low views, plus spill, shatter, settled glass and rewind. Settled glass remains transparent with soft edges/glints; the previous round blown-out floor hot spot is absent. Existing shard/puddle bloom caps remain.
